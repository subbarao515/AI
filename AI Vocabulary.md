# AI Vocabulary

## General AI Concepts

* **Prompt:** A prompt is the question or instruction given to LLMs (Large Language Models).
* **Tokens:** AI processes text in units called tokens. A token can be a single character, a word fragment, or an entire word.
* **Context Window:** AI memory; determines how much context it remembers (small and large context windows).
* **Fine-tuning:** An existing LLM model trained on a target dataset to adapt it for specific tasks.
* **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):** An approach to enhancing LLM performance by retrieving and providing relevant context from an external knowledge base.
* **AI Biases:** AI can inherit societal biases present in training data.
* **AI Hallucinations:** AI can confidently generate incorrect or fabricated information.
* **Narrow AI / Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI):** AI designed to perform a specific task or set of tasks (e.g., Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant).
* **General AI / Artificial General Intelligence (AGI):** AI designed to perform any intellectual task that a human being can—learning, thinking, inventing, and solving complex problems.
* **Singularity Point:** The point at which AI becomes superintelligent and surpasses human intelligence.
* **Super AI / Artificial Superintelligence (ASI):** A hypothetical type of AI that far surpasses human intelligence across all domains.
* **Responsible AI:** An approach to building AI systems that are fair, transparent, accountable, and safe.

## Large Language Models (LLMs)

* **Large Language Model (LLM):** A type of AI model trained on a large dataset of text and code. It can understand, process, and generate human-like text.

### Types of LLMs
* **General-Purpose Models:** Trained on broad internet data; suitable for a wide variety of tasks.
* **Specialized Models:** Trained or fine-tuned on domain-specific data for specific tasks.
* **Open-Source Models:** Freely available to use, inspect, and modify.

### Benefits of LLMs
* **Productivity Boost:** Automates repetitive tasks, freeing human time for strategic and creative work.
* **Scalability:** Scalable to handle large amounts of data and perform diverse operations.
* **Multilingual Support:** Understands and generates text in multiple languages.

### Cons & Limitations of LLMs
* **Hallucination:** AI can confidently generate incorrect or unverified information.
* **Bias:** AI can inherit and perpetuate societal biases from training data.
* **Resource Heavy:** Requires significant computational power and resources to train and run.

### Usages of LLMs
* **Content Creation:** LLMs can generate various forms of content, such as articles, marketing copy, scripts, and more.  
* **Customer Support:** LLMs can power chatbots and virtual assistants to handle customer inquiries and provide support.  
* **Knowledge Management:** LLMs can organize, summarize, and retrieve information from large knowledge bases.  
* **Code Assistance:** LLMs can assist developers by generating code, debugging, and providing code suggestions.  
* **Decision Support:** LLMs can analyze data and provide insights to support decision-making processes.
