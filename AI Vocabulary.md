# AI Vocabulary

* **Prompt:** A prompt is the question or instruction given to LLMs (Large Language Models).
* **Tokens:** AI processes text in units called tokens. A token can be a single character, a word fragment, or an entire word.
* **Context Window:** Its AI memory, how much context it remembers (Small and Large windows).
* **Fine-tuning:** An existing LLM model trained on a large dataset for your target dataset.
* **RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation):** It is an approach of enhancing LLM performance by providing relevant context from an external knowledge base.
* **AI Biases:** AI can inherit societal biases from training data.
* **AI Hallucinations:** AI can confidently generate incorrect information.
