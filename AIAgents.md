# What are AI Agents?

**AI agents** are virtual assistants that can understand a task, plan what to do, take action, check results, and deliver an answer. They are more than standard chatbots because they can work through a multi-step process and interact with external tools or data sources.

---

## 🔄 Core Workflow of an AI Agent

1. 📥 **Receive Task:** Receive a task from the user.
2. 📋 **Plan Actions:** Plan actions using its instructions and context.
3. ⚡ **Execute:** Execute the task using an LLM and, if available, tools or integrations.
4. ✅ **Verify:** Verify the result for correctness.
5. 💬 **Respond:** Respond with the final output.

---

## 💼 Why They Matter

AI agents help automate repetitive or multi-step work in IT and business tasks, such as:

* 📝 **Generating user stories**
* 🧪 **Creating test cases**
* 📦 **Summarizing releases**
* 👋 **Helping with onboarding**
* 🔍 **Reviewing code**

---

## 🧠 Key Idea

> **The LLM is the "brain" of the agent:** It interprets input, helps plan actions, and adapts dynamically based on feedback.
